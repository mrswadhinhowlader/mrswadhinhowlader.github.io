What the workflow
actually does.

Three builds, opened up: the trigger, the row that gets written, the alert that fires — and the point where a human takes over.

01 — Messenger

Inbound lead engine

A Facebook Page inbox that answers in seconds, qualifies the enquiry and puts the lead in front of the team, instead of leaving it buried in a chat thread nobody reopens.

  • Webhook trigger receives the message and identifies the sender
  • Intent routing sends FAQ answers or starts a qualification flow
  • Lead row written to Google Sheets with name, phone, service and tags
  • Telegram alert to the admin, with handover when a human is needed
n8n Messenger API Google Sheets Telegram
02 — WhatsApp

Order & support flow

A busy store's WhatsApp line: orders captured in one consistent format, customers told what happens next, and anything sensitive escalated with the conversation attached.

  • Structured conversation with quick replies and input validation
  • Order details stored, tagged by status and product
  • Automated follow-ups for pending and confirmed orders
  • Escalation rules route complaints to a human immediately
n8n WhatsApp Business Sheets / CRM Retries
03 — Content

LinkedIn realtime post generator

Give it a topic or a rough idea; it returns hooks, a structure and a first draft in the founder's own voice. Made for review, not for auto-publishing.

  • Idea intake through a simple form or chat message
  • Hook and structure generation from a curated template library
  • Draft delivered to the founder for approval
  • Approved posts logged so the pipeline stays consistent
n8n AI agent Sheets Approval step
