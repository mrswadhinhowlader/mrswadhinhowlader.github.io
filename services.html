Capabilities

Automation, built
to be trusted.

Six services, one standard: clean logic, stable execution and data your team can actually use. Every build includes testing, retries, logging and a written handover.

01 — Channel

Messenger automation

Your Facebook inbox answers instantly, asks the right questions, saves the lead and tells a human when the conversation needs one. Built on the Messenger Platform with n8n handling the logic.

  • Instant replies with smart FAQ and intent routing
  • Lead capture, qualification and clean data logging
  • Real-time admin alerts and smooth human handover
02 — Channel

WhatsApp Business automation

Structured chat flows that feel human and fast — for orders, support and follow-ups, with rules for when a conversation must go to the team instead of a bot.

  • Structured flows with quick replies and validation
  • Order handling, follow-ups, tags and status updates
  • Escalation rules for the team or admin when needed
03 — Alerts

Telegram bots & monitoring

The fastest way to know something happened. Leads, orders, payments and workflow failures arrive in Telegram the moment they occur — with the context needed to act.

  • Instant notifications for leads, orders and payments
  • Workflow monitoring alerts for errors, retries and failures
  • Admin signals and daily summaries for faster decisions
04 — Data

Google Sheets & CRM sync

One clean source of truth. Conversations become structured rows with consistent fields, tags and statuses — ready for reporting instead of manual cleanup.

  • Automatic capture into clean, structured rows
  • Customer records and pipeline tracking that stay updated
  • Report-ready exports and dashboard-friendly structure
05 — Plumbing

Webhook & API integrations

Your tools should talk to each other without a person in the middle. We connect systems through reliable pipelines, with retries and fallbacks so one failure does not break the chain.

  • Tool-to-tool connections through reliable webhook pipelines
  • API automations with retries, logging and fallbacks
  • Scaling without breaking systems that already work
06 — Content

LinkedIn realtime content systems

A content pipeline for founders who do not have hours to write. Strong hooks, proven formats and post structures generated from your own ideas and expertise.

  • High-quality hooks, formats and post structures
  • A consistent content pipeline for founders and teams
  • Template-driven generation with a smart scheduling flow
