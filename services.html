Capabilities

Automation that survives
a normal Tuesday.

Six services, one standard: it copes with messy input, it tells you when it breaks, and your team can maintain it without calling us. Testing, retries, logging and a written handover are included.

01 — Channel

Messenger automation

Your Facebook inbox answers instantly, asks the questions that actually matter, saves the lead and hands over the moment the conversation gets serious. Messenger Platform for the channel, n8n for the logic.

  • Instant replies with FAQ and intent routing
  • Lead capture with the fields your sales team keeps asking for
  • Admin alerts in real time, and a clean way to take over
02 — Channel

WhatsApp Business automation

Structured flows that still sound like a person — orders, support and follow-ups, with clear rules for the moment a conversation has to go to your team instead of a bot.

  • Quick replies and input validation, so orders arrive complete
  • Tags, follow-ups and status updates handled automatically
  • Escalation rules that route sensitive chats to a human
03 — Alerts

Telegram bots & monitoring

The fastest way to know something happened. Leads, orders, payments and workflow failures arrive as they happen, with enough context to act on them.

  • Instant notifications for leads, orders and payments
  • Monitoring alerts for errors, retries and failures
  • A daily summary, if you would rather not be pinged all day
04 — Data

Google Sheets & CRM sync

One source of truth. Conversations become structured rows with consistent fields and statuses — ready for a report instead of a cleanup session.

  • Automatic capture into clean, structured rows
  • Customer records and pipeline status that stay current
  • Exports that drop straight into a dashboard
05 — Plumbing

Webhook & API integrations

Your tools should talk to each other without a person in the middle. We connect them through pipelines with retries and fallbacks, so one bad response does not break the chain.

  • Tool-to-tool pipelines instead of manual exports
  • API calls with retries, logging and fallbacks
  • Room to add a system later without rebuilding the first one
06 — Content

LinkedIn realtime content systems

For founders who do not have three hours to write. Hooks and post structures generated from your own experience, then edited by you until they sound like you.

  • Hooks and formats drawn from what already works
  • A steady pipeline instead of a burst of posts every two months
  • Drafts land for approval — nothing publishes itself
Engagement

What working together
looks like.

Short and structured. Most builds follow the same four steps, whether it is one channel or five.

STEP 01

Map the process

We review how messages, leads and follow-ups move today — including the exceptions and the edge cases.

STEP 02

Build the workflow

n8n logic, API connections and data structure — built for real traffic, not a demo run.

STEP 03

Test the edges

Retries, failures, duplicate leads and handover are checked before anything goes live.

STEP 04

Hand over

Documentation, monitoring and a structure your team can follow without us.
