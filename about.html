Our work

We replace repeated
work with quiet systems.

We design and build end-to-end automation with n8n. A business replies faster, stays organised, and stops depending on someone remembering to follow up.

Most teams lose their day in small repeats: the same five questions, leads typed into a sheet by hand, reminders nobody sent, an admin pinged too late. Each one looks trivial. Together they set the pace of the whole business.

We replace them with workflows that run quietly in the background while the brand still feels responsive up front. The centre of the work is Messenger and WhatsApp Business: conversation flows that stay in tone, leads captured without asking the customer twice, and serious conversations handed to a person with the full context attached.

Around that we connect Telegram, email, Google Sheets and CRMs through webhooks and APIs — with logging, retries and clean data. An automation that fails silently is worse than no automation at all.

How we think

Three rules we build by.

Three rules that decide what we automate, what we leave alone, and what you get at the end.

A system, not a demo

It has to survive real traffic, half-typed messages and the same person writing three times. If it only works in a test run, it is not finished.

Humans stay in the loop

Automation takes the repeatable part and hands over the moment context matters. Escalation is designed in, not bolted on.

You own it afterwards

Clear node names, plain documentation, live monitoring. Your team can read the workflow, change a message and see what failed — without us.
